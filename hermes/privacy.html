Privacy Policy for hermes

Last updated: 2026-09-22

hermes is a personal automation assistant operated by Yoshitaka Yamazaki. This policy describes how hermes handles data obtained through Google APIs.

Data accessed

With the user's authorization, hermes may access Google Calendar data, including calendar event titles, times, locations, descriptions, attendees, and calendar metadata, to provide schedule summaries and related personal productivity features.

How data is used

Google data is used only to provide the requested personal productivity features. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to determine creditworthiness.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials and retrieved data are stored in the operator's private runtime environment and are not made public. Data is not shared with third parties except where necessary to operate an explicitly requested feature. No human reviews the data except with the user's affirmative direction for a specific task.

Retention and deletion

The user can revoke hermes's access at any time through their Google Account security settings. Stored OAuth credentials and cached Google data can be deleted from the runtime environment on request.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact Yoshitaka Yamazaki through github.com/yoshi65.
